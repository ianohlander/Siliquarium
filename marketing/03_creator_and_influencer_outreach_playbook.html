## 1. The Strategy: Intellectual Authority & Asymmetric Amplification

In technical and scientific software, cold spamming creators with generic PR copy gets deleted in 3 seconds. High-profile scientists and top-tier YouTube creators receive hundreds of solicitations a week.

To get their attention, you must achieve three things:

  1. 1. Show Radical Respect for Their Specific Intellectual Contribution: Reference their actual papers, books, equations, or video arguments—not just their follower count.
  2. 2. Present a Novel Scientific Realization: Demonstrate that your software solves or visualizes a problem they have publicly written or thought about for years.
  3. 3. Provide Frictionless Verification: Never ask them to "hop on a call" or "install a bulky application." Give them a 1-click in-browser URL where they can immediately witness the phenomenon running live in 5 seconds.

Here are the complete, ready-to-send outreach packages.

💻 TEXT 
Dear Professor Dawkins,

I am writing to share a computational biophysics milestone that was directly inspired by your writing over several decades, most notably "The Greatest Show on Earth" (2009) and your recent work, "The Genetic Book of the Dead" (2024).

In "The Greatest Show on Earth," you introduced the reality of natural selection by first grounding the reader in artificial selection—showing how human breeders (selective breeding) directed the transformation of wolves into domestic dogs and wild cabbage into broccoli and kale. You then posed the master question: if human breeders, operating over a fleeting few centuries, could achieve such radical diversification, what can blind nature achieve when operating over billions of years?

That conceptual progression is the exact structural backbone of this work:
- BooleanGA acts as the digital equivalent of selective breeding (directed evolution): human engineers define an engineering benchmark (a truth table), breeding digital logic until the algorithm synthesizes a fully functional, verifiable programmable 16-bit computer core (Hack ALU, sequential RAM latches, and instruction registers).

The reason I wanted to bring this to your attention is that it provides a direct, verifiable computational proof of your central thesis in "The Genetic Book of the Dead." 

As you articulated, an organism’s genome does not generate complexity ex nihilo; it is an inverse relief, a photographic negative, and a palimpsest of the ancestral environments in which its lineage survived. The environment is the informational fountainhead, natural selection is the recording channel, and the genome is the Book of the Dead.

In Siliquarium, when we examine an evolved organism through our Circuit Microscope and discover an AND gate, a Coherent Feed-Forward Loop, or an allosteric NOT inverter shielding the cell from chemical surges, that circuit did not arise by human design. It is a literal open page in the Genetic Book of the Dead—a physical, informational imprint proving that in ancestral epochs, chemical pulses surged in precise synchrony.

https://siliquarium.ianohlander.com

If you or your colleagues at the Foundation or the Center for Inquiry have five minutes to open the link, I hope you might enjoy seeing your ideas translated into living, running digital physics.

Thank you for a lifetime of intellectual clarity and inspiration.
