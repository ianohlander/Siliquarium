## 1. The Strategy: Intellectual Authority & Asymmetric Amplification

In technical and scientific software, cold spamming creators with generic PR copy gets deleted in 3 seconds. High-profile scientists and top-tier YouTube creators receive hundreds of solicitations a week.

To get their attention, you must achieve three things:
1. **Show Radical Respect for Their Specific Intellectual Contribution:** Reference their actual papers, books, equations, or video arguments—not just their follower count.
2. **Present a Novel Scientific Realization:** Demonstrate that your software solves or visualizes a problem they have publicly written or thought about for years.
3. **Provide Frictionless Verification:** Never ask them to "hop on a call" or "install a bulky application." Give them a 1-click in-browser URL where they can immediately witness the phenomenon running live in 5 seconds.

Here are the complete, ready-to-send outreach packages.
