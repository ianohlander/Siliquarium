## 🎓 Educational Curriculum & Laboratory Practicums

The **Siliquarium Curriculum** is structured as an advanced 4-unit course designed for 12th-grade STEM seniors and undergraduate students in biophysics, computer science, and systems biology.

- **[Unit 1: The Prebiotic Threshold & The Epistemic Cut](CURRICULUM_AND_SYLLABUS.html#unit-1-the-prebiotic-threshold--howard-pattees-epistemic-cut):**  
  Explores Howard Pattee's separation between symbolic code (the 1D genome tape) and dynamic physical law (active logic gates). Covers Peter Mitchell's proton motive force, non-equilibrium redox chemistry, and abiotic energy harvesting.
- **[Unit 2: The Logic of Life — Logic Gates as Catalytic Enzymes](CURRICULUM_AND_SYLLABUS.html#unit-2-the-logic-of-life--logic-gates-as-catalytic-enzymes):**  
  Investigates how digital logic functions as metabolic machinery. Understand `AND` gates as co-substrate catalysis, `NOT` gates as allosteric feedback inhibition, and Rolf Landauer's thermodynamic switching cost.
- **[Unit 3: Systems Biology & Network Motifs](CURRICULUM_AND_SYLLABUS.html#unit-3-systems-biology--network-motifs):**  
  Introduces Uri Alon's network motifs in living circuits: bistable toggle switches, ring oscillators (repressilators), and feed-forward loops. Teaches topological graph isomorphism using the Weisfeiler-Lehman algorithm.
- **[Unit 4: Macro-Evolutionary Dynamics & The Lenski LTEE](CURRICULUM_AND_SYLLABUS.html#unit-4-macro-evolutionary-dynamics--the-lenski-ltee):**  
  Examines Motoo Kimura's neutral drift, Susumu Ohno's gene duplication, convergent evolution (homology vs. homoplasy), and pelagic spore dispersal in benthic fluid currents.

## 💡 Quick Start: Launching the Simulation

To launch and run the interactive Siliquarium laboratory on any standard web browser:

1. **Start the local server** (if running locally):
   \`\`\`bash
   npm run dev
   \`\`\`
2. **Open the laboratory:** Navigate to \`http://localhost:3000\` in your browser.
3. **Orbit the 3D Seafloor:** Click and drag with the left mouse button to rotate; right-click and drag to pan; use the scroll wheel to zoom.
4. **Inspect a Silicoid:** Click directly on any glowing hexagonal pore on the seafloor to lock the camera and activate the **In-Situ 3D Reticle** and **Circuit Microscope HUD**.
5. **Observe Circuit Dynamics:** Watch active logic gates (`AND`, `NOT`, `OR`, `XOR`) pulse cyan and gold in real time as the organism converts hydrothermal chemicals into metabolic energy.
