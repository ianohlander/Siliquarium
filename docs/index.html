A Teacher's Orientation

The Unsupervised Sea: Why Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamics Must Precede Loss Functions

Welcome, student of nature and computation. If you open almost any computer science textbook on artificial intelligence or evolutionary algorithms, you will be greeted by a sterile world of mathematical shortcuts: artificial fitness functions, predetermined optimization benchmarks, and teleological target bitstrings. While these engineering tools are useful for building ALUs or sorting arrays (the mission of our sister project, BooleanGA), they smuggle the very secret they claim to explain: how purposeful life emerges spontaneously in an indifferent physical universe.

In natural biophysics, life does not compute an external score; it extracts free energy from non-equilibrium chemical and thermal gradients, maintains its internal state against Rolf Landauer's thermodynamic dissipation ($k_B T \ln 2$), and reproduces before its battery runs dry. Information is not a floating number in memory; it is a physical, rate-independent polymer governed by quantum chemistry and enzyme kinetics.

Most fundamentally, information is not invented ex nihilo by the genome. As evolutionary biologist Richard Dawkins (2024) demonstrates in The Genetic Book of the Dead, and as theoretical physicist Christoph Adami (2000) proved mathematically, an organism’s genome is an archival palimpsest and an inverse photographic negative of its ancestral environment. The environment is the informational fountainhead; natural selection is the recording channel; the genome is the Book of the Dead.

Siliquarium is a standalone autopoietic computational laboratory. Here, digital circuits are freed from engineering truth tables and placed into a deep-sea hydrothermal vent matrix. Primitive catalytic enzymes (AND, OR, NOT) and emergent composite circuits (XOR) convert chemical plumes into metabolic battery tokens. This documentation suite is your comprehensive companion: master the biophysical foundations, explore the interactive 3D seafloor, and witness the spontaneous genesis of living silicon circuits.

When observing Siliquarium, a new explorer might ask: "Is this an abstract video game, or an authentic simulation of real physics and biology?"

Siliquarium is an authentic computational biophysics laboratory. However, instead of attempting to compute trillions of continuous water molecules and freezing after three picoseconds, Siliquarium abstracts physical reality into rate-independent symbolic code and rate-dependent digital components under strict thermodynamic conservation laws ($\Delta E \equiv 0.000, \Delta M \equiv 0.000$).

🔒 1. The Safe & The Workshop (Epistemic Cut)

Analogy: A master cookbook in a fireproof safe versus a bustling kitchen workshop. You never chop up the cookbook recipe to fry an egg!

Nature vs. Sim: DNA is inert, non-catalytic, and protected; enzymes do the messy, rate-dependent work. In Siliquarium, GenomeSafe stores a quiescent 60-bit tape (zero current, zero runtime mutation). The active PoreWorkshop expresses CMOS logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, XOR) that burn Landauer calories and charge battery tokens.

🌋 2. The Vent (Digital Drumbeat & Redox Flux)

Analogy: Digital food is not a stationary bowl of soup; it is a passing musical drumbeat. Catch the beat to charge your battery!

Nature vs. Sim: Deep-sea vents emit oscillating fluxes of electron donors ($H_2$) and acceptors ($CO_2$). In Siliquarium, VentPhysics emits periodic bitstreams: Stream A (fuel), Stream B (oxidizer), and Toxin T. Circuits co-catalyzing $(A \land B)$ harvest $+2$ free energy tokens per cycle.

🪨 3. The Rock Pores (Nature's First Walls)

Analogy: Free stone houses sheltering proto-life from being washed away and diluted before lipid membranes evolved.

Nature vs. Sim: Russell & Hall (1997) proved porous $FeS$ rock foams prevented the dilution catastrophe ($C \to 0$). In Siliquarium, HexGrid3D provides physical honeycomb cavities $(q, r, z)$. Rock walls confine cells, while open fluid cells allow spore dispersal.

🔋 4. The Battery (Landauer Heat & Mitchell Gradient)

Analogy: A cellular calorie counter. Toggling switches burns calories; matching vent beats charges calories; zero calories means death.

Nature vs. Sim: Mitchell's proton motive force ($\Delta p \sim 200\text{ mV}$) and Landauer's thermodynamic bound ($k_B T \ln 2$). In Siliquarium, PoreBattery tracks $[0..100]$ tokens. Gate flips cost $-1$ token in heat dissipation; $100\text{T} + 40\text{M}$ triggers division; $0\text{T}$ triggers autolysis.
