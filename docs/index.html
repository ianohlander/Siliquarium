🎓 Educational Curriculum & Laboratory Practicums #

The Siliquarium Curriculum is structured as an advanced 4-unit course designed for 12th-grade STEM seniors and undergraduate students in biophysics, computer science, and systems biology.

  • Unit 1: The Prebiotic Threshold & The Epistemic Cut:

Explores Howard Pattee's separation between symbolic code (the 1D genome tape) and dynamic physical law (active logic gates). Covers Peter Mitchell's proton motive force, non-equilibrium redox chemistry, and abiotic energy harvesting.

  • Unit 2: The Logic of Life — Logic Gates as Catalytic Enzymes:

Investigates how digital logic functions as metabolic machinery. Understand AND gates as co-substrate catalysis, NOT gates as allosteric feedback inhibition, and Rolf Landauer's thermodynamic switching cost.

  • Unit 3: Systems Biology & Network Motifs:

Introduces Uri Alon's network motifs in living circuits: bistable toggle switches, ring oscillators (repressilators), and feed-forward loops. Teaches topological graph isomorphism using the Weisfeiler-Lehman algorithm.

  • Unit 4: Macro-Evolutionary Dynamics & The Lenski LTEE:

Examines Motoo Kimura's neutral drift, Susumu Ohno's gene duplication, convergent evolution (homology vs. homoplasy), and pelagic spore dispersal in benthic fluid currents.

🧪 Hands-On Interactive Laboratory Practicums #

Each unit culminates in an experiential laboratory practicum conducted directly inside the Siliquarium application:

  1. Practicum 1: Hydrothermal Micro-Survey & Circuit Inspection — Navigate the 3D seafloor, inspect pore micro-architecture, and observe real-time circuit execution.
  2. Practicum 2: Landauer Dissipation & Metabolic Starvation — Quantify gate switching penalties and observe cell death when energy reserves deplete.
  3. Practicum 3: Phenotypic Adaptation to Vent Chemical Dynamics — Track evolutionary tuning across pulsed chemical streams A and B.
  4. Practicum 4: God-Suite Perturbations & Ecological Resilience — Induce thermal shocks and extinction pulses to measure ecosystem recovery and lineage survival.
  5. Practicum 5: Lenski LTEE Dynamics & Retrospective Flight Recorder Autopsy — Reconstruct ancestral trajectories through genotype-phenotype manifolds without lag.

💡 Quick Start: Launching the Simulation #

To launch and run the interactive Siliquarium laboratory on any standard web browser:

  1. Start the local server (if running locally):

\\\`bash

npm run dev

\\\`

  1. Open the laboratory: Navigate to \http://localhost:3000\ in your browser.
  2. Orbit the 3D Seafloor: Click and drag with the left mouse button to rotate; right-click and drag to pan; use the scroll wheel to zoom.
  3. Inspect a Silicoid: Click directly on any glowing hexagonal pore on the seafloor to lock the camera and activate the In-Situ 3D Reticle and Circuit Microscope HUD.
  4. Observe Circuit Dynamics: Watch active logic gates (AND, NOT, OR, XOR) pulse cyan and gold in real time as the organism converts hydrothermal chemicals into metabolic energy.
